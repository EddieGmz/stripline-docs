RF-E

Riverflux Audio

27/36shared controls
17own controls
9not on this plugin
Riverflux Audio RF-E, an E-series channel strip. PRE is the DRIVE, LINE the line input; TRIM (after the fader), the plugin's own INPUT and OUTPUT, and MIX are IN's small knobs. The low pass, the compressor threshold and both mid Qs run the other way in the plugin: inverted. TYPE switches the EQ between Brown and Black. The compressor has a FAST attack switch and no makeup gain; the gate's MODE steps through Expand, Gate 1 and Gate 2, so there is no separate expander switch. DYN is the whole dynamics section, EXT the external key, LSTN key listen, OS the oversampling. The fader is the plugin's own fader (-inf to +10 dB).
