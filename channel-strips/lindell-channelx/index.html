Knobs pass the plugin's own position through; the console prints the plugin's text. No gate and no fader. Only the normal EQ is on the strip (its Mid and Side copies stay in the plugin; M/S picks the mode). The compressor has no threshold: its Input drives it, on THRSH inverted. ATTACK and RELEASE are the continuous knobs, active when CONT is on. ORDER: P(re), C(omp), E(Q) in signal order.
