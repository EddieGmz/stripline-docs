Scheps Omni Channel 2

Waves

Knobs pass the plugin's own position through; the console prints the plugin's text. Stereo and Mono (name match). Only the main (left / stereo) set of controls is on the strip; the R copies for Duo and M/S modes stay in the plugin, as do the de-esser, the limiter and the inserts. EQ: LOW, TONE (on LO MID), MID (on HI MID), HIGH; their filter types are the EQ buttons. Gate range is the Floor knob, inverted.
