Analog Obsession FrankCS (Ultimate Channelstrip). The low band is Pultec-style: Gain is the low BOOST, its ATTEN is on the EQ's small knobs. The plugin's MID band is the low-mid band here (75 Hz to 12.5 kHz). High atten and its frequency are small knobs too (HATT, HFRQ); there is no low pass. Attack, release and ratio click through the plugin's steps. The compressor has no makeup gain. TUBE buttons switch the preamp (IN) and the compressor model; OS is oversampling. The EQ and COMP buttons are the plugin's "EQ Bypass" and "Compressor Bypass" parameters, which are 1 when the section is on. The fader is the plugin's Output (-24 to +24 dB).
